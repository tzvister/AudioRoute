Welcome to AudioRoute

Get the mix you want for your calls, lessons and recordings. Combine your microphone, instrument and app audio, then tell Claude Code or Codex what each person should hear.

What will be installed?

  • The AudioRoute app in your Applications folder.
  • A command for your agent to create and adjust your mixes.
  • An audio driver and a small background service that provide virtual microphones and speakers for apps such as Zoom.

Your Mac will ask for your password to allow installation. Install from the account you plan to use; AudioRoute currently supports one account per Mac.

You’ll need macOS 14.2 or later. After installation, restart your Mac to get everything ready. Save any calls or recordings before restarting.

You can uninstall AudioRoute at any time. Download the uninstaller from AudioRoute’s GitHub releases. Your saved setups will be kept for a future reinstall.
