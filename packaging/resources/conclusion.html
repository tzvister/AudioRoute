AudioRoute is installed

Restart your Mac when convenient so Core Audio can load the installed driver. This installer has not interrupted your audio sessions.

Open Terminal and run:

/usr/local/bin/audioroute setup

Start the routing service with audioroute setup --start. The first route that uses a microphone or application audio may trigger a macOS privacy prompt for AudioRoute. Approve only the capture you intend to use.

For virtual-device consumers such as a meeting app, select the named AudioRoute virtual microphone in that app's audio settings.
