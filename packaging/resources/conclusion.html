You’re ready to make your mix

First, restart your Mac. This finishes loading AudioRoute’s audio driver.

Then open Claude Code or Codex on this Mac and ask:

Run audioroute setup --start, check that AudioRoute is ready, and help me set up my audio.

Describe what you want—for example, “My guitar teacher should hear my guitar and voice, and I should hear my guitar and the teacher.” Your agent can build the mix and help you select its virtual microphone and speaker in Zoom.

Your Mac may ask for microphone or app-audio access when you use those sources. Allow the sources you want to include, then test the sound with your agent.

Want to remove AudioRoute later? The uninstaller download removes the installed components and keeps your saved setups.
